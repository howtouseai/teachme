Welcome to Teachme Learning Assistant

Thank you for installing Teachme Learning Assistant! This extension helps you understand complex content by explaining it to you and then asking follow-up questions to ensure comprehension – just like the famous Feynman Technique.

How It Works

1

Extract Content

Navigate to any webpage with content you want to understand, click the extension icon, and press "Extract Page Content".

2

Listen to Explanation

Click "Explain Content" to hear an AI-generated explanation of the content in simple terms.

3

Answer Follow-up Questions

The assistant will ask you questions to check your understanding, helping you identify any gaps in your knowledge.

Key Features

Content Extraction

Automatically extracts the main content from web pages, filtering out menus, ads, and other distractions.

Audio Explanations

Converts complex content into clear, spoken explanations that are easy to understand.

Feynman Technique

Uses the proven learning method developed by Nobel Prize-winning physicist Richard Feynman.
