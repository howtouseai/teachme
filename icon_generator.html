How to Use These Icons

  1. Download the generated icons (16x16, 48x48, and 128x128 pixels)

  3. Move the downloaded icons to the images folder
  4. Rename them to icon16.png, icon48.png, and icon128.png
  5. Your extension will now display these icons in Chrome
